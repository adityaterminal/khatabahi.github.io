About Khata Bahi

Platform Overview and Vision

Khata Bahi is a digital bookkeeping and business-management utility created to make everyday record keeping simpler, more organized, and easily accessible from modern web browsers.

Small retail counters and micro-enterprises often balance cash flow, vendor dues, customer credit, and GST documentation manually across paper books. Khata Bahi brings these records into an integrated, offline-first digital environment backed by cloud sync.

Our Core Purpose

The platform is designed specifically for shopkeepers, traders, freelancers, and independent service providers who need rapid, clutter-free record management without heavy enterprise bloat.

What Khata Bahi Delivers

  • Digital Ledger: Rapid debit (Udhar) and credit (Jama) recording.
  • Gullak Micro-Savings: A dedicated personal cash reserve tracker.
  • Multi-Branch Profiles: Independent bookkeeping across multiple outlets.
  • Document Conversion Suite: In-browser PDF, Excel, and image processing tools with zero third-party data transmission.
  • Invoice Generation: Instant print-ready commercial tax and service invoices.
