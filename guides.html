Khata Bahi — Complete User Guide

Standard Operating Procedures & Feature Instructions

1. Managing Store Branches

Set up independent ledgers for each counter or branch:

  1. Click the Branch Outlet selector in the top bar or under Store Profile.
  2. Select Register New Store and enter the business title.
  3. Enter store address, GSTIN, and PAN details in the profile section so that generated invoices carry accurate header credentials.

2. Adding Ledger Entries

Click + Add Entry to record transactions:

  • Receipt: Money received from customers (Jama).
  • Payment: Money disbursed for vendor bills or shop expenses (Udhar/Kharach).
  • Sale / Purchase: Commercial vouchers with line items, quantity, units, and custom GST breakdown rates (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).

3. Using Gullak (Micro-Savings)

Gullak is an isolated digital piggy bank for setting aside spare counter change or daily profits. Deposits and withdrawals do not skew your main commercial profit/loss register.

4. Document Utilities

All file utilities (PDF to Excel, Excel to PDF, Image Compression) execute completely inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. Files are not sent to any external server.

5. Data Backup & Cloud Synchronization

To enable real-time cloud synchronization across devices, sign in with your Google account. Regularly use the Export button to maintain offline `.csv` backups of your ledgers.
