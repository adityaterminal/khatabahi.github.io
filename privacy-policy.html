Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Khata Bahi, user privacy is paramount. This platform allows retail businesses, traders, and freelancers to record accounting ledgers, tax invoices, and micro-savings records.

1. Local & Cloud Storage Architecture

By default, all transaction entries, store parameters, and Gullak savings entries are stored locally on your device via browser `localStorage`. When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, records synchronize with Google Firebase/Firestore isolated strictly to your authenticated account UID.

2. Client-Side Document Processing

Khata Bahi features document utilities (PDF-to-Excel, Excel-to-PDF, and photo compression). These utilities process documents purely within your browser's local sandbox memory; no customer files are transmitted or stored on remote servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Cookies

We may use third-party tools to maintain infrastructure and security:

  • Google Firebase: Identity authentication and encrypted Firestore synchronization.
  • Google AdSense: Serves contextual advertising to support the free tier.

4. Data Deletion

Users can delete local storage at any time by clearing browser history and application data. To request cloud deletion of synchronized records, contact us directly.
